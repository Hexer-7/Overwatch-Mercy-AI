# Improvements

- Flash Heal is pressed only while Mercy holds her staff. With the blaster out,
  on another hero or with a menu open, its key is left alone, so it never
  reloads the blaster by mistake.
- The staff is read from the weapon icon beside the ammo counter, and works
  over bright backgrounds and sun glare at every resolution.
- On by default. Turn it off under Settings > Advanced > Flash Heal >
  Only with the staff out.
