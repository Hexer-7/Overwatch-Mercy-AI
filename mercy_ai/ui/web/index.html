Two things about your game

Mercy AI reads your ally’s health ring off the screen. It only reads reliably if the game looks the way it expects.

Set the health bar to white
Options → Video → Colour Blind → set health, shield, armour and overhealth to white. Blue is the default and it will not match.
Run Overwatch fullscreen
Not borderless. Borderless lets Windows shift the picture and the capture lands in the wrong place.

Pick two spare keys

In Overwatch, bind heal and damage boost to keyboard keys as well as the mouse. Mercy AI presses those keys — it can’t press your mouse buttons.

Those are the same key. Heal and damage boost need to be different, or they’ll fight each other in game.
Choose any keyboard keys you like. Not a mouse button.

Three things to set in Overwatch, then hold the trigger
1White health bars
Options → Video → Colour Blind → set health, shield, armour and overhealth to white. Blue is the default and it will not match.
2Fullscreen, on the main monitor
Options → Video → Display mode → Fullscreen, not borderless. Overwatch must run on your primary monitor.
3Give Mercy’s beam two keys
Options → Controls → Mercy: add your heal key () as a second key for Primary Fire and your damage boost key () for Secondary Fire. Keep the mouse buttons too. You choose the keys under Settings.
This computer
Nothing happens in game

Mercy AI asks for administrator rights when it starts, because Windows silently drops key presses sent to a game that runs with higher rights. If you answered No, close it and open it again. Otherwise check the three steps above, and that the resolution under Settings → Advanced matches your screen.

More in Mercy AI
  • Text version. Everything in this window also works in a console, with the arrow keys and Enter: run "Mercy AI.exe" -cli.
  • Calibration. "Mercy AI.exe" -calibrate shows your ally’s health match live while you play, to help you choose the switch point under Settings.
  • Checks. -monitors lists your screens, -benchmark times the screen reading. -help lists every option.
  • How to run these. Press Open folder below, click the address bar of that folder, type cmd and press Enter, then type the command.
  • Reset. A blue Reset appears beside any number or key you changed; it puts back the default.
  • Advanced. Settings → Advanced has the trigger, pause key, Flash Heal and more. Test changes there in the Practice Range first.
Mercy AI
Version · free and open source

A pocket-healing buddy for Mercy. It watches your ally’s health ring, holds heal while they’re hurt, and slips into damage boost the moment they top off — so you can watch the fight instead of your beam.
